Privacy Policy — Davey Health

Effective November 10, 2026.

Davey Health is a personal-use application operated by Alex Dowlen, an individual, for his own use only. Its only user is Alex himself. This policy describes what data the app accesses and how that data is handled.

Data accessed

With Alex's authorization through Google's OAuth consent flow, Davey Health reads the following metrics from Alex's own Google Health account, which is fed by his own Fitbit device:

  • Breathing rate during sleep (respiratory rate)
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2)
  • Heart-rate variability
  • Resting heart rate
  • Weight

Access is read-only, under the single scope health metrics and measurements (read-only). The app does not write to, modify, or delete anything in the Google Health account.

Purpose

The data is used for one purpose: Alex's personal health tracking. His personal assistant software uses it to prepare his own sleep and health summaries and to watch for unusual patterns in his own numbers over time.

Storage

OAuth credentials and the retrieved data are stored on Alex's own computer. Credential files are stored with restricted file permissions. The data is not uploaded to any third-party service by this app.

Sharing

The data is never sold. It is never shared with third parties. It is never used for advertising, marketing, or profiling. No one other than Alex has access to it through this app.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only on Alex's own systems, under his control, and he can delete it at any time.

Revoking access

Alex can revoke the app's access at any time:

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions while signed in.
  2. Select "Davey Health" in the list of third-party apps with account access.
  3. Choose "Remove access."

Once access is removed, the app can no longer read any data.
