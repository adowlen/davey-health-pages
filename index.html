Davey Health

Davey Health is a personal-use application owned and operated by one individual, Alex Dowlen, for his own use. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

What it does

Davey Health connects Alex's own Google Health data — recorded by his own Fitbit — to his personal assistant software, which runs on his own computer. With his authorization, the app reads a small set of his health metrics so the assistant can include them in his personal sleep and health summaries.

Data the app reads

  • Breathing rate during sleep (respiratory rate)
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2)
  • Heart-rate variability
  • Resting heart rate
  • Weight

Access is read-only. The app reads only Alex's own data, from his own account, with his consent.

How the data is handled

The data is processed on Alex's own systems for his own personal health tracking. It is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising.
